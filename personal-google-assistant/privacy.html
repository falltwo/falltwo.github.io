隱私權政策

「falltwo 個人郵件與行事曆助理」只供我本人使用。我授權自己的 Google 帳號後，工具才會讀取資料。

讀取與使用

工具會查看兩個 Gmail 帳號中新郵件的寄件者、主旨和時間。只有在判斷郵件是否需要處理時，才進一步讀取內文或同一串對話。行事曆功能只查看指定帳號的行程資訊，用來整理提醒。

這些資料用於本人查看重要郵件、產生摘要和期限提醒，不用於廣告或資料販售。Google Workspace 權限取得的資料只用於上述本人可見的功能，並遵守 Google API Services User Data Policy 的 Limited Use 要求。

資料會到哪裡

工具在我管理的個人環境中執行，Google 授權憑證保存在該環境。為產生摘要，必要的郵件內容可能送往目前使用的 AI 模型服務（OpenAI Codex）；重要事項的摘要可能送到我的 Discord 頻道。這些服務會依各自的條款處理收到的內容。本工具不以 Google 資料訓練一般用途的 AI 模型。

摘要與處理紀錄可能留在本機對話、排程紀錄及備份中；送出的通知也會留在 Discord。資料不會自動於固定日期刪除。

停止使用與刪除

我可以從 Google 帳號設定撤銷授權，停止日後的讀取。撤銷授權不會自動清除先前的本機紀錄、備份或 Discord 訊息；要移除這些資料，需另外清理各自的儲存位置。

Privacy Policy

falltwo Personal Mail & Calendar Assistant is for my own use. It accesses my Google data only after I authorize my accounts.

Data accessed and purpose

The tool checks the sender, subject, and time of new messages in two Gmail accounts. It reads a message body or thread only when needed to decide whether an item requires attention. It also reads events from one selected calendar account to prepare reminders.

This data is used to identify important mail, prepare summaries, and remind me of deadlines. It is not used for advertising or sold. Data obtained through Google Workspace scopes is used only for these user-facing functions, in accordance with the Limited Use requirements of the Google API Services User Data Policy.

Processing and storage

The tool runs in an environment I manage, where Google authorization credentials are stored. Necessary message content may be sent to the AI service currently in use (OpenAI Codex) to generate a summary. Summaries of important items may be sent to my Discord channel. Those services handle received content under their respective terms. This tool does not use Google data to train general-purpose AI models.

Summaries and processing records may remain in local chat history, scheduled-task records, and backups. Notifications sent to Discord remain there as well. There is no fixed automatic deletion date.

Revocation and deletion

I can revoke Google access in my Google Account settings to stop future access. Revocation does not automatically remove earlier local records, backups, or Discord messages; these must be deleted separately from their respective storage locations.
