falltwo Personal Mail & Calendar Assistant

This is a tool I use for my own accounts. It checks new messages in two Gmail accounts for items that need a reply, action, or deadline reminder. It also reads calendar events from one of those accounts for reminders.

The tool runs in an environment I manage. When something needs my attention, it sends a short summary to my own Discord channel. It does not offer account registration or mailbox access to anyone else.

Message content may be sent to an AI service to produce a summary. See the privacy policy for how the data is accessed, stored, and shared.
